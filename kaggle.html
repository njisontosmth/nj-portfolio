01 / COMPETITION CLOSED · RETROSPECTIVE

Kaggriculture — Autonomous AI Farming Agent

Designed and evaluated Python agents that make decisions about crops, labour, expansion, livestock, resources and profit in a competitive farming simulation. My role included defining strategy changes, reviewing replays, evaluating candidate performance and deciding which experiments to retain, with AI-assisted coding and analysis.

4,960Final rank (self-reported)
6,162Earlier V1 rank (self-reported)
1,035.6Historical score · 23 Sep 2026

Experiment journal

V1 · Diagnose the crop-only baseline

Replay review identified planting and expansion bottlenecks, excess unused seeds and fixed worker allocation. Reordered priorities and explored land acquisition.

V2–V3 · Expand the economy

Explored growth, diversification, competitor observation, livestock, feed and fertiliser management.

V4–V5 · Improve decision quality

Refined cash-flow logic, production timing and worker routing; explored demand and opponent forecasting, adaptive strategy modes and look-ahead. Synthetic checks passed; full-game superiority was not established at that stage.

C95 · Benchmark against public code

Adopted a public Apache-2.0 strategy by Rayk Kretzschmar as an attributed benchmark. Used it to compare candidates rather than presenting the original strategy as my own.

V6–V8 · Test safeguards and stability

One offline V6 candidate passed 800 randomised tests but was not submitted. V8 completed 20/20 comparison games without errors; all tied C95, demonstrating stability rather than improvement.

E2 series · Reject weak changes

Emergency feeding disrupted productive actions: E2-M4 lost all 12 games against each of two benchmarks. E2-M6 initially won 8 of 12 games but won only 8 of 20 held-out games. These candidates were rejected rather than submitted as improvements.

Lessons

Test changes against strong baselines; separate correctness from competitive improvement; use held-out comparisons; preserve attribution; keep unsuccessful experiments as evidence. The reported final rank is not independently verified, and the historical score is not a final score.
